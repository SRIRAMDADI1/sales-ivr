# Coverage objection playbook

When the caller questions whether coverage is sufficient:

- Restate coverage limits clearly.
- Explain what is included and excluded.
- Offer tier upgrade path without pressure tactics.
- Flood and earthquake are separate from homeowners, condo, renters, and landlord policies.
- A personal umbrella sits above auto and home. It does not replace them.
- Private workers compensation is not offered in North Dakota, Ohio, Washington, or Wyoming.
- Standardized Medicare supplement is not offered in Massachusetts, Minnesota, or Wisconsin.
- Trip, wedding, event, builders risk, crop, and surety prices are one-time premiums.
